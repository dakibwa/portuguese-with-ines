Inês's wordmark and the site's icons, from `public/` and `src/app/` in the repository.

- `wordmark-cream.webp` is the master: her lettering of "Português com a Inês" in cream on transparency, 760 × 236 artwork. The site never shows it as an image: it is a mask (`--wordmark-image`) painted `blue` on cream (header) or `paper` on blue (footer), with the two circumflexes cut out as their own layers (see the Wordmark card). On a page here it shows cream, so view it on a dark tile.
- `icon.png` is the browser-tab icon (`src/app/icon.png`): a cream-and-lavender splat with a coral accent on dark blue, replacing an earlier flower.
- `og.png` is the picture every shared link shows (WhatsApp, Messages and the like), 1200 × 630: the open-centre lavender splat on the right of one continuous `blue-deep` ground, with clear space round it and its tips intact. The words come with the link's title, never in the picture.
- Never redraw the wordmark, set the name in Beth Ellen instead, recolour it, or place a mark beside it.

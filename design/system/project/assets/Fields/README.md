The large painted fields that pages are laid on, and the paper's grain, from `public/visuals/`.

- `cream-blue-fan-splat.webp` is How I teach's fan, beside the heading; `-mobile` is its phone crop (under 560px). The site also serves AVIF versions of both.
- `open-centre-lavender-splat.webp` is the Lessons hero's splat on its blue panel, and the shared-link picture's; `-mobile` is its phone crop.
- On the page, fields stay still with their tips clear of the edges and lettering. On the share card, the lavender petals run off the top and right edges.
- `paper-grain.svg` is the grain over every ground (`--paper-texture`), cream pages and blue bands alike. A 240px tile of black fractal noise at 7% alpha (an SVG filter), so it darkens any ground evenly; it tiles.

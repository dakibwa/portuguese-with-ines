# ConfirmBooking

The last step: the choices and chosen lessons on the left, and one card on the right that confirms them.

**Markup:** `.booking-confirmation-columns`: the booking bar and `.booking-chosen-lessons` beside `.booking-final` (`Confirm your lessons`, the note, PaymentFacts, Agreement, the final action).

- Headed simply `Confirm your lesson` or `Confirm your lessons`, weekly ones included: the bar already says they repeat.
- The note is one line tall, like a field, until a note needs more; it grows as typed up to a limit and can be resized.
- Payment facts sit under the note, then the agreement, then the final action, all one width. The agreement chosen turns blue beside the coral action, so two coral fills never meet.
- The final action names what it does: `Book 2 lessons & agree to pay`. Until it can act it is the quiet outline.
- On a phone everything stacks in one column in the same order.

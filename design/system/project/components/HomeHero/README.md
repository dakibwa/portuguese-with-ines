# HomeHero

The home page's first screen: a blue panel with the title, one line, Book a lesson and two ways in, beside three principles on a lilac rail.

**Markup:** `<section class="home-hero"><div class="home-hero__copy"><h1><span>Portuguese</span><span class="home-title__script">Lessons</span></h1><div class="editorial-rule"></div><p>…</p><div class="home-hero__actions"><a class="button button--coral">Book a lesson</a><nav class="home-hero__links">…two button--outline-light button--compact…</nav></div></div><section class="principles-strip">…three article.principle…</section></section>`

- Copy panel `blue` with the grain; title `display-hero` in `paper`, its second word `lavender`, italic; line in `hero-lead`.
- The principles take the right of the hero above 900px, stacked on a soft-lavender rail (`lavender-soft`), each a splat (76px), a `display-principle` heading in `lavender-ink`, a `short-rule` and one `copy-small` line. At 900px and below they follow the blue panel in the same order.
- The three actions share one row where they fit; where they don't (under 38.25rem of the panel), Book a lesson spans the pair beneath it. On a phone the pair sits side by side, a size smaller, rather than adding a row above the fold.
- Never the business-card artwork here (the "business-card transplant").

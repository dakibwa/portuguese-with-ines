# PastLessons

Lessons already had, newest first, under the calendar in Your lessons.

**Markup:** `.past-lessons`: the `Past lessons` legend, then a `raised` tile per lesson (`.past-lesson`) with its date and time, its status (`Cancelled` in coral with its mark) and length · place; `6 earlier lessons` opens the rest.

- Each lesson is a `raised` tile with its mark, the long date (breaking before its year, never leaving the time alone on a line) and a small status pill (`Paid`, `Charged`, `Paid by hand`) that keeps the pill's own size.
- Three to a row where they fit; one on a phone.

## Write

Drawn by `PastLessons` (`src/components/PastLessons.tsx`) in a student’s Your lessons: change it there.
